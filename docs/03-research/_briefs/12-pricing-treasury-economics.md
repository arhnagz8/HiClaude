# Brief 12 — pricing-treasury-economics (stage-2 synthesis; runs AFTER specialists 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 10 have delivered)

**Goal:** Turn the research facts into the definitive, quantified economics for the platform: cost stacks, price ladders, margin targets, float/working-capital model, constraint-bound capacity, capital requirements, FX exposure, sensitivities — and recommended default parameters for the pricing engine and treasury planner.
**Inputs:** `docs/03-research/{01,02,03,06,07,08,10}-*.md`, `data/{providers,exchanges,regulatory_limits,gateways,collection_methods,catalog,competitors,fx_history,macro_calibration,ops_growth}.json` (Record format: unwrap `.value`), `docs/05-architecture/architecture.md` §7–§9, `packages/contracts/src/{params,defaults}.ts`, `scripts/pricing_model.py` (first pass).
**Deliverables:** `docs/03-research/12-pricing-treasury-economics.md`, `data/config/pricing.json`, `data/config/treasury.json`, `data/config/regulatory.json` (plain values matching `PlatformParams` slices — see `packages/contracts/src/params.ts`; each file also has a sibling `_provenance` block mapping key → source record path / research doc), `scripts/research/12_economics.py` (re-runnable, reads data files, prints tables), `scripts/research/12_tables.json` (machine-readable outputs).

## Checklist
1. **Cost stack per product family** (virtual card issue, card top-up, vouchers via API, service payments): provider fees (issue, top-up %, fixed, FX), exchange taker fee + spread, network/withdraw fees (allocated per order), gateway/c2c/bank fees, SMS/identity costs, support/operator minutes × wage, refund/decline/fraud allowances. Show per $25/$50/$100/$500 and per payment method. All formulas explicit.
2. **Price ladders**: compute prices at USDT/IRT = spot, +10 %, +20 %, +40 %, −10 % for each family and method; show margin %, markup vs competitor median (from 07), and where the competitor guard binds.
3. **Margin targets by family** from competitor markup distributions (07), elasticity and speed-premium willingness; recommend `marginPct`, `floorMarginPct`, `minMarginIrt`, `rush` tiers (premium %, min premium, capacity) and `usdt.marginPct`; justify each with data; report expected conversion impact.
4. **Volatility/lag buffer calibration**: from `fx_history`/`macro_calibration` (08) compute daily σ and drift by regime; evaluate buffer formula `z·σ·√τ + μ·τ_lag` over history to measure **coverage of realised adverse moves** (backtest: share of quotes where the realised replacement-cost move within lock+lag exceeded the buffer) for z ∈ {1.0,1.3,1.64,2.0,2.33}; pick z/min/max; quantify the margin cost of safety. Include night-halt/OTC-gap handling.
5. **Float & working-capital model**: required USDT float vs daily demand under (a) 72 h lock, (b) 48 h, (c) 24 h, (d) none; effect of ID deposit caps (25M Toman/24 h × K identities) and daily buy caps (2,000 USDT) on **maximum sustainable daily GMV**; provider float cap interplay; table of capital needed to sustain 20 / 50 / 100 / 300 orders per day at AOV $60; inventory holding **FX gain/loss** under drift scenarios (hold vs just-in-time; exposure per day of coverage); breakeven drift where holding float pays vs fees.
6. **Fixed-cost model** (10): staff (ops/support/dev), tools, hosting, marketing, legal/accounting, taxes; breakeven orders/day for 3 cost structures; payback on 50M / 300M / 1B Toman starting capital under base/optimistic/pessimistic demand — include ranges not points.
7. **FX shock sensitivity**: ±20 % in 3 days with/without buffer; inventory gain/loss and margin squeeze/windfall; recommended policy (when to widen buffer, pause rush, re-quote cadence).
8. **Payment method economics**: net margin per method (gateway vs c2c vs USDT-direct vs wallet) incl. risk allowances; where USDT-direct discount is affordable.
9. **Recommended defaults** written as plain-value JSON slices that validate against the zod schema (`PricingPolicySchema`, `TreasuryParamsSchema`, `RegulatoryParamsSchema`); also list items you could not calibrate (UNVERIFIED) so the owner verifies first-hand.
10. A **one-page owner summary in Persian** at the top of the Markdown: price formula in words, margins, capital needed, daily limits, main risks.

Use Python for every calculation (show formulas). Guardrails: lawful scale only (business accounts, higher KYC tiers, OTC, real partners with consent); never model splitting payments or borrowed identities as a strategy — if the binding constraint is a regulatory cap, say what lawful options raise it (e.g. business/OTC accounts) or that growth is capped.
